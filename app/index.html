A few editing essentials

Import a file, add it to a track, then select its clip. Set source in/out in the inspector to trim, or press S at the playhead to split. Drag clips to arrange them.

Save JSON to keep your edit. Media files stay on your device: reimport the same files after reopening a project.

AI, multitrack rendering, transitions and MP4 export need the separate engine.
